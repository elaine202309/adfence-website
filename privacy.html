Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Draft — pending review. This policy describes the app as it is published on the App Store, but it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Check every statement against the shipping build before relying on it.

AdFence is a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It blocks advertising, hides on-page interruptions such as cookie notices and chat widgets, and can block adult websites. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • AdFence has no user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • The extension does not record the pages you visit, and does not send them anywhere.
  • There is no AdFence server receiving your browsing activity, because there is nothing for it to receive.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information — we don't hold any to sell.

What the extension does on your device

AdFence works through Apple's Content Blocker framework. The app supplies Safari with a set of pre-compiled rules describing what to hide. Safari — not AdFence — evaluates those rules as each page loads, on your device.

Because Safari applies the rules itself, the extension is never handed the contents of the pages you read, your browsing history, your searches, or the addresses you type. It has nothing to store, and nothing to transmit.

Information we do not collect

AdFence does not collect, and has no technical means of collecting:

  • Your browsing history, the URLs you visit, or page contents
  • Searches, form entries, passwords or any other data you type into a website
  • Your name, email address or Apple Account details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files on your device

Data stored on your device

AdFence keeps your preferences — which filter lists are switched on, and any per-site exceptions you have set — in local storage on your device. These settings are yours and stay there. Removing the app removes them.

If you have iCloud enabled, Apple may sync app settings between your own devices as part of its standard device backup and sync behaviour. That syncing is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Purchases

If AdFence offers a paid upgrade, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple may provide developers with aggregate, anonymised sales reports, which do not identify individual users.

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not have a login, a comment system or a contact form that stores data. If you email us, we receive your message and your email address, and we use them only to reply.

As with any website, the hosting provider processes standard technical request data — such as your IP address and browser type — in server logs in order to serve the page. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Third parties

AdFence does not embed third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs in the app. We do not share information with third parties because we do not hold information about you to share.

Children

AdFence is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since the app collects no personal information from any user, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Privacy laws in some regions — including the GDPR in Europe and the CCPA in California — give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because AdFence does not collect personal data, there is no data for us to retrieve or erase on your behalf. If you believe we hold information about you, write to us and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
