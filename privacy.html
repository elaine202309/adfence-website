Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

AdFence is a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It blocks advertising, hides on-page interruptions such as cookie notices and chat widgets, and can filter adult websites. This policy explains what information AdFence processes, what it does not process, and the choices you have. It applies to the AdFence app and to this website.

The app is published by an independent developer. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean that publisher, and “you” means the person using the app or visiting this site. We are the data controller for the processing described below. Our contact address is support@mxcode.com.

Summary

  • AdFence has no user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • The extension does not record the pages you visit and does not transmit them anywhere.
  • We operate no server that receives your browsing activity, because there is no activity for it to receive.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we hold none to sell.

The summary is provided for convenience only. The sections that follow set out the full position and take precedence over it.

1. How AdFence works

AdFence is built on Apple's Content Blocker framework. The app supplies Safari with a set of compiled rules describing which page elements to hide. Safari — not AdFence — evaluates those rules as each page loads, on your device.

Because Safari applies the rules itself, AdFence is never given the contents of the pages you read, your browsing history, your searches, or the addresses you type. There is no stage at which that information reaches the app, so there is nothing for the app to store or transmit.

AdFence does not send your browsing history, page contents, or any information about how you use Safari to us or to any third party. It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app has no technical means of collecting:

  • your browsing history, the addresses of pages you visit, or the contents of those pages;
  • searches, form entries, passwords or any other data you enter into a website;
  • your name, email address, or Apple Account details;
  • location data, including precise or coarse location;
  • contacts, photos, calendars, health data, or files on your device;
  • identifiers used for advertising or cross-app tracking, such as the advertising identifier;
  • usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports.

3. Information stored on your device

AdFence stores your preferences on your device: which filter categories are switched on, any per-site settings or exceptions you have created, and your custom rules. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, Apple may include app data in your device backup as part of its standard backup and sync behaviour. That processing is carried out by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us. You can manage or disable iCloud Backup in your device settings.

4. Purchases through the App Store

AdFence is distributed through the App Store, and any purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details, your billing address, or your Apple Account information. Apple may provide developers with aggregated, de-identified sales reports, which do not identify individual users. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

5. This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no login, no comment system, no contact form, no advertising and no tracking pixel.

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. It runs only after you press Accept on the banner shown on your first visit; until then nothing is sent to Google and no cookie is written. While it is running, Google Analytics receives the page you opened, the site you arrived from, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and the usual technical details of the request — browser, language, screen size. It sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_3GFDY4TL3R, which hold a random identifier for your browser and expire after two years. None of it is connected to the app, to an Apple Account or to anything that identifies you, and we only look at it in aggregate.

Google processes this data on our behalf and may do so outside your country; Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with Cookie settings in the footer — the banner will ask again and analytics stays off if you decline. Google also publishes a browser opt-out add-on.

Like any website, the hosting provider processes standard technical request data — such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested — in order to deliver the page and to protect the service. That processing is carried out by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us, we receive your message, your email address and anything you choose to include in it. We use that information only to read and answer your message, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

6. Legal bases and your rights

Because AdFence collects no personal data, we do not process personal data in order to operate the app. Where you contact us by email, we process your email address and the contents of your message on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to you, and we keep that correspondence only for as long as it is needed for that purpose.

Privacy laws in some regions — including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and the California Consumer Privacy Act — give people rights over their personal data, including rights to access, correct, delete or port it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data about users of AdFence, there is no such data for us to retrieve, correct or erase. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will investigate and respond.

We do not sell or share personal information within the meaning of the CCPA, and we do not discriminate against anyone for exercising a privacy right.

7. Data retention

We retain no personal data from your use of the app. Correspondence you send us is retained only as long as necessary to resolve your enquiry and to keep a record of the outcome, after which it is deleted.

8. International transfers

We do not transfer personal data internationally, because we do not collect it. The App Store and this website are operated by Apple and GitHub respectively, and each may process technical data in countries other than your own under its own privacy terms and transfer safeguards.

9. Security

AdFence does not maintain a database of user information, so there is no store of personal data that could be exposed in a breach. Your settings are protected by the security of your device and of iOS, iPadOS or macOS, including the device passcode and, where enabled, device encryption and iCloud security features. You are responsible for keeping your device and its passcode secure.

10. Children

AdFence is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and because it collects no personal information from any user, it collects none from children. The adult-website filter is a convenience feature, not a parental-control or child-safety product, and it is not presented as one.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Where a change is material — for example, if a future version of the app begins to collect any information — we will also describe it in the app's release notes before or when the change takes effect.
