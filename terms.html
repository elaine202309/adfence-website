What AdFence is

AdFence is a Safari content-blocking extension. It supplies Safari with filter rules that hide certain page elements. It is a filtering tool, not a security product or a parental-control system.

What we don't promise

  • It won't block everything. No content blocker stops all advertising. Ads served from a site's own domain, and video advertising delivered with the video itself, are generally out of reach.
  • It may occasionally break a page. Filtering can hide something a website needs. If a site stops working, turn AdFence off for that site.
  • It isn't a security control. AdFence is not antivirus, not a VPN, and not a firewall. Do not rely on it to protect you from malware or fraud.
  • It isn't parental control. The adult-content filter has no passcode and can be switched off by anyone with access to Safari's settings. For restrictions that can't be bypassed, use Apple's Screen Time instead.

Availability

AdFence works in Safari on iOS, iPadOS and macOS. Safari extensions are sandboxed by Apple and cannot operate inside other browsers or the in-app browsers of other applications. This is a platform limit we cannot work around.
