Setup

On in about a minute

Safari extensions have to be switched on by hand. Here's the whole process.

Install the app

Download AdFence from the App Store. It installs on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Allow the extension

Open Settings → Safari → Extensions and switch AdFence on. On a Mac, do it in Safari's settings.

Keep browsing

That's it. Pages load without the ads, the banners and the pop-ups.

Works in Safari only. Safari extensions are sandboxed by design — an ad blocker can't reach into other apps' in-app browsers. This is the same limit every Safari ad blocker has, and it's what keeps the extension unable to see your browsing.

Questions

Before you ask

Is AdFence available now?

Yes. It's on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac — get it here.

Do I need to pay for it?

The App Store listing shows the current price, if there is one. There is nothing to buy on this site.

Does it work in Chrome, Firefox or in-app browsers?

No. AdFence is an ad blocker for Safari, so it only works in Safari on iOS, iPadOS and macOS. It can't reach the in-app browser inside other apps such as Instagram or Facebook — no Safari extension can, because Apple sandboxes them.

Does it slow Safari down?

No. A Safari content blocker hands Safari a pre-compiled list of rules, and Safari applies them itself as the page loads. There's no extra processing running on every request.

Do you collect my browsing data?

No. There's no account and no browsing history stored. See the privacy policy for the details.

Will it break websites I use?

Aggressive blockers sometimes hide something a page needs. If a site misbehaves, you can turn AdFence off for that site in Safari's extension settings and leave it running everywhere else.
