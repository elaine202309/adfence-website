Getting AdFence working

Almost every "it isn't blocking anything" report is the extension not being switched on. Start here.

Enabling the extension

Don't have it yet? Download AdFence on the App Store, then follow the steps for your device.

On iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Scroll down and tap Safari
  3. Tap Extensions
  4. Tap AdFence, then turn it on
  5. Allow access for the websites you want it to work on

On Mac

  1. Open Safari
  2. Go to Safari → Settings (or Preferences)
  3. Open the Extensions tab
  4. Tick AdFence in the list
  5. Choose which sites it can access

If it isn't working

Ads still show up on a site

A few possibilities, in the order worth checking:

  • The extension is on, but not allowed on that website — check Safari's extension permissions for the site.
  • The ads are served from the site's own domain. Those are hard to tell apart from real content, so blockers generally leave them alone.
  • It's a YouTube in-video ad. Those are delivered with the video itself and are largely out of reach for any content blocker.
  • The filter list is behind. Updating the app updates the lists.
A page is broken — content missing or a menu won't open

Aggressive blocking can occasionally hide something a page needs. Turn AdFence off for that one site in Safari's extension settings and leave it on everywhere else.

Cookie banners are still appearing

The annoyance filters have to be switched on separately from the ad filters. Check the toggles in the app.

Nothing is blocked anywhere, at all

The extension isn't enabled. Follow the setup steps above — Safari extensions are off until you turn them on by hand.

Can I unblock a single site?

Yes. In Safari's extension settings you can set AdFence to ask per-site, allow it everywhere, or deny it everywhere, and then make exceptions for individual sites.
